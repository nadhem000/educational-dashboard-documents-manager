How it works: This tool splits audio into 6 overlapping frequency bands. Each band isolates different sound categories — sub‑bass rumble, footsteps, male voices, general speech, bird calls, and high‑frequency insects/small birds. Use SOLO to preview a single band, MUTE to silence one, and adjust the frequency cutoffs and volume to fine‑tune isolation. Volume now goes up to 10× for weak signals. Click the ⬇ Export button on a band to download that band's isolated audio as a WAV file (after rendering with your current settings). Frequency‑based filtering works best when sounds occupy distinct frequency ranges.
